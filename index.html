Vision, Mission & Goals

What drives KMPC forward.

Our shared commitment guides every decision and action we take.

⊕

Our Vision

A financially stable and a recognized service-oriented cooperative with quality members.

◎

Our Mission

Enhance business enterprise through mutual help to serve its members and community.

▸

Our Goals

  • To generate fund and to grant loans at an affordable rate.
  • To engage in credit and productive endeavors.
  • To mobilize resources for community development.
