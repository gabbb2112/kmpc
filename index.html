Vision, Mission & Goals

What drives KMPC forward.

Our shared commitment guides every decision and action we take as a cooperative.

Our Vision

A financially stable and recognized service-oriented cooperative with quality members and a strong community presence.

Our Mission

Enhance business enterprise through mutual help to serve its members and the wider community with excellence.

Our Goals

  • Generate funds and grant loans at an affordable rate.
  • Engage in credit and productive endeavors.
  • Mobilize resources for community development.
Core Strategy

Three pillars for sustainable member growth.

Our strategy balances financial strength, member support, and community investment for the long term.

I

Financial Growth

Flexible savings options, member loans, and shared returns make it easier for families to prosper through cooperative finance.

  • Member loan support
  • Flexible repayment plans
  • Transparent dividend sharing
II

Community First

Community programs, educational workshops, and local partnerships help strengthen the region and uplift every member.

  • Workshops and training
  • Local outreach events
  • Member-first governance
III

Future-Ready

Modern cooperative tools and transparent governance ensure sustainable, resilient operations for generations to come.

  • Digital member services
  • Clear financial reporting
  • Long-term resilience planning

Our Commitment

The Cooperative Pledge

A declaration of unity, responsibility, and shared purpose that guides every KMPC member each day.

Cooperative Pledge of the Philippines

A Pledge of Unity & Purpose

As a Filipino I am and I believe,
in the cooperative,
alone I am weak,
But with others, I am Strong. So I commit myself,
To work, to cooperate
for all to be prosperous. Harmony, Industry, I will value,
Cooperative affairs, I will attend,
Responsibilities, I will assume.
The cooperative philosophy, I will live. One Vision, One Belief,
One feeling in cooperativism
My life I pledge,
So help me God.
KMPC Est. 1997 Iligan City
